# ApkPy showcase applications

These are the complete Python files behind the public Lumen, Onda,
Northline and Afterglow case studies: the app, its styles, and nothing else.

| App | Entry file | Screens | What it shows |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Lumen | `lumen_finance.py` | 4 | gradient cards, charts, a list drawn from rows, switches |
| Onda | `onda_wellness.py` | 4 | words over a picture, rings, chips, a dark screen in a light app |
| Northline | `northline_travel.py` | 4 | a photo hero, cards in pairs, a boarding pass, a plan |
| Afterglow | `afterglow_music.py` | 4 | a dark theme, covers, gradient tiles, a player |

The pictures are in `art/`. They are drawn by `make_art.py` (Pillow and
NumPy), so nothing in them was downloaded or generated by an image model.

To rebuild an app, copy its entry file to a project as `writehere.py`, copy
the `art/` folder next to it, then run `apkpy build` or `apkpy run`.

Signed APKs of the four apps, and their SHA-256 sums, are on the
[showcase page](https://repo-apkpy.pages.dev/showcase/).
