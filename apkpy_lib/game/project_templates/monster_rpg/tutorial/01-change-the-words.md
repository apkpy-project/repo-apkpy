# 1. Change the words

The story is plain text. Open `story/village.story`: every `say` line is something on the screen.

In `story/village.story`, change `The tide went out nine days ago.` to `It all started with a letter.`

Now the money. How the game starts is in `data/game.json`: the first map, the starters and more.

In `data/game.json`, change `"currency": "shells"` to `"currency": "coins"`

Press **New journey** in the game. You see your first line, and your money is in coins.
