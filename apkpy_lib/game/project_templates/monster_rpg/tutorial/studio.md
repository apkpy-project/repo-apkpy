# The Studio

The Studio is one window for making your game. Everything it changes is in ordinary files of this project, so you can use it, a text editor, or both.

```
apkpy game studio
```

## A first hour

Each of these takes a few minutes. Press **Play** at any time to try what you did.

1. **My game.** Change the name, and the name of the money.
2. **Title and icon.** Write your game's name and press **Draw it**. Pick a monster for the icon.
3. **Story.** Change what the first lines say. The file `village.story` has them, in the event `intro`.
4. **Monsters.** Press **New**, give it a name, and choose its types and stats. It gets a drawing when you save. To change it, press **Draw it in Art**: it opens the drawing in **Art**, and draws one first if it has none yet. **+ Add a variant**, at the bottom, gives it another look, such as a rare golden one: choose how rare it is in the wild, and which trainers have it in **Trainers**.
5. **Art.** Find your monster, and draw over it. Its front is the first cell, and its back the second.
6. **My game** again. Make your monster the first of the **Starters**.
7. **Maps.** Open `village`. Pick `person` from the objects, click a free cell, and press **Write what happens...**. Write what they say.
8. **Maps** again. Pick `house_blue`, and click a free place with a free cell below its middle. Press **New map...** for its inside, add an `exit`, and write the new map's name in the house's **inside** field.
9. **Test.** Under **Forgotten**, see what you may have missed. Under **World**, see your maps.
10. **Publish.** Connect your phone, and press **Install it**.

## The sections

| Section | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Play** | The real game, in this window |
| **Test** | Start anywhere with any team, try fights, see the balance, the world, and what you forgot |
| **My game** | Name, version, how it starts |
| **Maps** | Paint maps, and place people, houses and links between maps |
| **Story** | What people say, and what happens |
| **Monsters**, **Moves**, **Types**, **Items**, **Shops**, **Trainers**, **Wild monsters**, **Natures** | The content of the game |
| **Art** | Draw or import every picture |
| **Title and icon** | The title's word, the phone's icon, the font |
| **Music and sound** | What each place sounds like and looks like behind |
| **Publish** | Install on your phone, and make a file to share or to upload |
| **Copies** | Go back to an earlier state of your project |

## Good to know

- **Saving.** A `*` beside a section means it has changes. **Ctrl+S** saves everything.
- **Undo.** **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y** work in the section you are in.
- **Problems.** The line at the very bottom checks your saved files. Click it, then click a problem to go there.
- **Copies.** They are made by themselves, before anything big. You can always go back.
- **Your signing key.** The first signed version makes a key that only you have. Every update needs that same key, so press **Save a copy of the key** in **Publish** and keep the copy safe.
