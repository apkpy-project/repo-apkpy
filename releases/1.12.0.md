# ApkPy 1.12.0 — what a finger does

Write native Android apps in the supported Python subset. The generated APK
still contains Java/XML, not a Python runtime.

1.11.0 rebuilt the screens of apps everybody knows. Rebuilding the screens
around them -- a profile, an inbox, a settings page, a sign-up form, a sheet
of filters -- showed what was still missing: tabs that swipe, charts, rows
that swipe away, chips, a field that says what is wrong with it. This release
is those. Each is drawn by the Previewer and by the phone from one set of
rules, and each was used on a phone before it was written down here.

## New

- **Tabs that swipe: `pager()` and `tab()`.** A profile's Posts / Reels /
  Tagged, a chat app's Chats / Status / Calls. Each `tab()` is a page, and
  what it holds goes inside it with `parent=`. A tap or a swipe slides to a
  page, the indicator follows the finger, and `tabs.select(2)` does it from
  code. On the phone it is Material's TabLayout over a pager added only to
  apps that use one.
- **Charts: `chart(items, kind="bar" | "line" | "donut" | "ring")`.** Drawn
  natively -- a Canvas on the phone, the same shapes in the Previewer -- with
  an axis that ends on a round number, labels thinned when they would not
  fit, a legend under a donut, and `on_click(item)` for the bar, point or
  slice that was tapped. Rows from `db.query()` work as they come. No chart
  library is added to the app.
- **Rows that swipe and move.** `virtual_collection(..., swipe_right=swipe(
  "archive", "Archive", on_swipe=archive, undo="Archived"), on_reorder=moved)`.
  A swipe past 40% takes the row away; with `undo=` a snackbar offers to bring
  it back and your function only runs once it has gone unpressed; `keep=True`
  slides the row back. A long press lifts a row and a drop calls
  `on_reorder(item, index)`. Every gesture is also an accessibility action on
  the row.
- **Chips and segmented buttons: `chips()` and `segmented()`.** Options that
  stay selected, one at a time or with `multiple=True` any number;
  `required=True` for a filter bar that always keeps one. `on_change(value)`
  gets the word or the list. 48dp to the finger, read by a screen reader as a
  checkbox or a radio button, kept through a rotation.
- **Fields with help and errors.** `inputs(..., help=, error=, required=)`,
  `field.set_error(text)` and `validate(name, email)`, which shows every empty
  required field at once, puts the cursor in the first and returns whether
  they passed. Typing clears the error.
- **A bottom sheet with components in it.** `bottom_sheet(..., screen=home)`
  holds whatever a container holds -- chips, fields, buttons -- given
  `parent=<the sheet>`. It is still Material's bottom sheet on the phone: the
  drag, the scrim, Back and the keyboard are the platform's. `on_close=` is
  called whichever way it closed. In the Previewer every sheet now has round
  corners and a handle and drags down to close.
- **A picture that opens and zooms.** `image(src, zoom=True)` opens on a tap
  over the whole window: two fingers zoom up to four times, a double tap
  zooms in on the spot and back, a drag moves a zoomed picture, and Back, the
  cross or a drag down closes it. `view_image(src)` opens any picture from a
  function.
- **`back()`.** Closes this screen for the one that opened it, which comes
  back as it was. A back arrow written `on_click_navigate(previous)` started
  a new copy of the previous screen, and the system's Back gesture then walked
  through the copies.
- **`on_change` on a switch, a checkbox and a slider.** It was read for text
  fields only: a settings switch moved and nothing heard it.
- **A grid that scrolls with its screen:** `height: auto` on a
  `virtual_collection`, for a profile's photo grid under its header.
- **Examples:** [`34_sign_up_form.py`](../examples/34_sign_up_form.py),
  [`35_filter_sheet.py`](../examples/35_filter_sheet.py) and
  [`photo_viewer/`](../examples/photo_viewer/). Lumen's Insights tab is charts
  now, and [`replicas/`](../examples/replicas/) gained a profile with tabs and
  a photo grid, settings, an inbox whose rows swipe and a task list that
  reorders.

## Fixed

On the phone:

- On Android 15 and later, a plain screen -- no scroll, no bottom bar -- lost
  its padding, and its content touched the edge of the glass.
- A `container` with no background of its own painted the page's colour: a
  band across the card or sheet it sat on.
- A column with `align-items: center` centred its children on the widest one,
  against the left edge; text that wrapped inside a flex container was
  centred; a photo cropped with `object-fit: cover` drew outside its box.
- A button with a small `height` cut the bottom off its words.
- `for item in ITEMS:` inside a function did not compile when `ITEMS` was a
  list written at the top of the file.
- A modal, sheet or menu could not be written below the function that opens
  it.

In the Previewer, where the phone was already right:

- A screen's `gap` did nothing; a screen with no padding had none at the
  sides; `padding: 0px` kept 12px on top.
- A grid of component rows spaced its cells 10px apart; `grid()` covers were
  150px squares; `flex-grow` on a button in a row did nothing; an empty
  container with a `height` was 1px tall; a caption over a picture sat in the
  middle of its box; a switch had 16px more at each side.
- A password field showed its hint as asterisks.

On both:

- A checkbox's `get_value()` was the bool `True` in the Previewer and the text
  `"true"` on the phone.
- The accessibility report (`U2035`) never looked inside a row built from
  components.

## Upgrading

Most apps generate what they generated before. These can change what you see:

- **In the Previewer, a checkbox answers `"true"` or `"false"`**, as on the
  phone. Write `agree.get_value() == "true"`.
- **`on_change` on a switch, a checkbox or a slider is called now.** If you
  passed one and it never ran, it runs.
- **Screens with a `gap`, or with a `Theme`, are more spaced in the
  Previewer.** It is what the phone always showed.
- **On the phone, a container without a background is transparent**, a column
  with `align-items: center` is centred in its box, wrapped text starts at
  the start, and a button with a `height` from the stylesheet centres its
  words in it.
- **A build can stop where it used to pass**, each time naming the line:
  `U2040` to `U2046`, one for each new component given something it cannot
  build.
- A function of your own called `back` still wins over the new one.
- Regenerate the Android project.

## Verified

- 1,713 feature tests, 35 unit tests and 258 transpiler checks pass. Charts,
  chips, fields and the picture viewer each keep their arithmetic in one
  Python module, written again in Java for the phone; a test compiles that
  Java with `javac` and puts the same questions to both.
- 79 example and test apps transpiled by 1.11.0 and by 1.12.0 with a
  deterministic sweep (`tools/sweep_apps.py`). The generated files differ in
  54 of them, and every changed line was read: the root of a plain screen
  (29 apps, the Android 15 padding), a container's background, wrapped text
  and cropped pictures in flex containers, and Lumen's charts. Five apps
  that 1.11.0 refused -- they use the new components -- now build.
- **On a phone** (Xiaomi, Android 16), driven through `adb` and photographed:
  the pager (a drag to the next page, a tap on a tab, a short drag springing
  back); the four charts, a tap on a bar and a ring moving from 72 to 91%;
  a swipe half way and back, a swipe with Undo, a delete, a reorder and
  `keep=True` twice on the same row; chips filtering a chat list, a music
  home and an inbox; a form showing two required fields at once, clearing as
  it is typed in, and the cursor in the first wrong field; a sheet opening
  with its components, read by its Apply button, closed by a drag, a tap
  outside and Back, and pushed up by the keyboard; the picture viewer opened
  from a large picture and from a thumbnail, double-tapped, dragged and
  closed three ways; `back()` through three screens with no copies left on
  the stack.
- The four showcase apps rebuilt as signed 1.12.0 releases with the same
  keys: `aapt2` confirms each one's package, `versionName` and launcher
  Activity after R8. **On the phone**, each was installed over its 1.11.0
  build and every tab opened: Lumen's four charts draw and a tap on May's
  bar reads "May: $1,530"; Onda, Northline and Afterglow look as they did.
  The rebuilt apps were built again with the final engine and their
  profile, settings, inbox (a swipe half way) and task list seen once more.

## Not verified

- TalkBack reading the gestures, the fields, the sheet and the viewer. The
  actions and roles are there and were read with `uiautomator`; nobody
  listened to them.
- The form, the sheet and the viewer through a rotation.
- The viewer's two-finger pinch -- `adb` has one finger. It shares its
  arithmetic with the double tap, which was seen.
- A picture opened in the viewer from a URL.
